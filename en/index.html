The property

Spacious, flexible and highly accessible

This substantial industrial building at Inductorstraat 41, on the Het Ambacht/Nijverkamp business park in Veenendaal, is becoming available for lease. Its location close to the Rondweg-Oost gives the property excellent accessibility.

The property comprises generous industrial space on the ground floor, combined with high-quality office space spread over the ground and first floors. Its functional layout makes the building suitable for a wide range of business activities, including heavy industry (environmental category 4.2, a Dutch zoning classification), production, storage and logistics.

The industrial space features, among other things, ten sliding doors, three-phase power, a concrete floor and roof lights. Changing rooms and a canteen with pantry are also present. The offices are neatly finished and benefit from plenty of natural light thanks to the large windows.

Generous industrial hall

Approx. 5,380 m², approx. 70.5 × 85.3 m in five 15 m bays, height 7.5 m. Crane track by arrangement.

High-quality offices

Approx. 1,390 m² over the ground and first floors, with air conditioning, LED lighting and opening windows.

Heavy-duty connections

183 kW contracted electricity transport capacity and 45 m³/h gas. Plant room with compressors.

Strong logistics position

Directly on the Rondweg-Oost (N233), with quick access to the A12 and A15. Ample parking on the property's own grounds.
